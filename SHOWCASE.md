## Credits & process

These projects adapt existing character assets. Original models, textures, rigs and characters remain the work of their respective creators and rights holders. Leatherface uses assets from the TCN game; Halloween supplies target character assets and the rig. Jason X uses the unreleased skin from F13.

This gallery contains rendered media, not source models or a playable mod download. Published downloads and installation instructions belong on Nexus.


This presentation was created with the help of AI ngl. The studio renders use the existing geometry and textures, without AI image generation.
